2. The apps on your device

To-do, Notes, Calendar, Mail and Contacts store their data on your device. Without signing in, none of it is sent to us. In the Windows app, Mail and Contacts connect directly from your device to the email and contacts providers you add (for example Microsoft or Google); those messages and contacts are not sent to Alondra1, except copies you choose to keep in the Mail Archive (section 4). The Web app keeps its data in your browser's storage on your device.

3. This website

Delivery and security

When you visit this website or use the Web app, your browser sends technical data such as your IP address, the page requested, the time, and your browser's user agent. This is necessary to deliver the pages and protect them against abuse (legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure, working website, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The sites are served through Cloudflare (content delivery, DNS and protection against attacks) from servers of Hetzner Online GmbH in Germany.

Analytics, only with your consent

If, and only if, you choose Accept in the cookie banner, this website loads Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) to measure how the site is used. Before you decide, and after you decline, no Google code is loaded and no request is sent to Google. Analytics sets the cookies _ga and _ga_<id> (kept for up to two years) and processes your shortened IP address, device and browser details and the pages you visit. Google may process data in the United States; transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; § 25(1) TDDDG).

Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage (alondra1-consent, not a cookie) for 12 months. You can change it at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page; declining removes the analytics cookies. The development site (alondra1.dev) never uses analytics.

4. Account and cloud features

If you sign in, we process the following to provide the cloud features you use (legal basis: performance of our contract with you, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR):

  • Account: your email address, the time the account was created, and your spaces and their members.
  • Sign-in: one-time codes sent by email. We store only a keyed hash of each code; it is valid for 10 minutes and works once. Sign-ins, devices (the name and platform you or the app give them) and the time they were last used. Rate limits use a keyed hash of your IP address, never the address itself.
  • Cloud Sync: the tasks, lists, notes, note pictures, calendar events and settings you choose to sync, so that they appear on your other devices.
  • Mail Archive (paid plans): copies of the email messages you choose to archive, so you can search them later.
  • Plans: the plan of each space and any beta access we granted. There is no online payment yet; if that changes, this policy will name the payment provider before it is used.

Synced data is stored in a database on servers of Hetzner Online GmbH in Germany. Note pictures and archived messages are encrypted before they are stored, in Hetzner Object Storage in Germany. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS). The cloud is not end-to-end encrypted: our service can read synced data to provide the features, and only authorized operators may access it, for operating and securing the service.

Sign-in codes and space invitations are sent by email through {{emailProvider}}. Technical logs record request types, timings and errors; they do not contain your content, sign-in codes, tokens or full email addresses.

5. How long we keep data

  • Synced items stay until you delete them. Notes in Trash are deleted for good after 30 days.
  • When you delete your account, your email address is removed at once and your sessions end; your content is purged within 30 days. Deleted spaces are purged after 30 days.
  • Encrypted backups of the database and stored files are kept for up to 35 days, so deleted data disappears from backups at the latest 35 days later.
  • Security and administration events (without content) are kept for one year.
  • Analytics data is kept according to the retention set in Google Analytics (at most 14 months).

6. Who processes data for us

We use these service providers, bound by data processing agreements:

  • Hetzner Online GmbH, Germany: servers, database storage, encrypted backups and object storage (in Germany).
  • Cloudflare, Inc., USA, and its EU affiliates: content delivery, DNS, protection against attacks, access control for our administration pages, and storage of public configuration files (in the EU). Transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.
  • {{emailProvider}}: delivery of sign-in codes and invitations.
  • Google Ireland Limited: website analytics, only with your consent (section 3).

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with anyone else unless the law requires it.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, for example {{supervisoryAuthority}}.
